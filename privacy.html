Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Charles Coach is a personal app used only by its owner, Amir Baja.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, it reads (read-only) health data from his Google Health / Fitbit account: activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements, sleep, workout location, nutrition, reproductive health, logged symptoms, mindfulness, ECG and irregular rhythm notifications.

How the data is used

Only to generate short daily health summaries for the owner. The data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens and summaries are kept only as long as needed to provide the summaries. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
